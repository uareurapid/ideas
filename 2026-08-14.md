## General Ideas (5)

**Idea 1: LostLink**
Most lost & found is still a cardboard box behind a front desk with no photos, no search, and no way to get items back. LostLink is a simple platform where venues (gyms, airports, bars, transit stations) snap a photo of each found item and people log what they lost, with photo matching and one-tap "claim + pick up" handoff.

**Idea 2: MedDrop**
People have no idea where to safely dispose of expired or leftover medication, so pills end up flushed or hoarded. MedDrop maps drug take-back locations and pharmacy disposal bins near you, sends expiry reminders, and tells you what's flushable vs. what must be returned.

**Idea 3: GearGo**
Traveling parents either haul cribs, car seats, and strollers through airports or pay resorts a fortune to rent them. GearGo is a peer-to-peer rental marketplace where locals rent out baby/kid gear by the day, with cleaning checklists, safety standards, and insurance baked in.

**Idea 4: SeatSaver**
Planning wedding or event seating is a manual spreadsheet nightmare, and nobody remembers who can't sit next to whom. SeatSaver is a drag-and-drop table planner with conflict detection ("don't seat Aunt M near the DJ"), auto-balancing, and one-click export of place cards.

**Idea 5: HouseManual**
Every home has hidden knowledge — where the shut-off valve is, which breaker controls what, when the filters were last changed — that's lost when someone moves. HouseManual lets you build a digital owner's manual for your home and hand it off to the next owner or tenant, so they never have to guess.

## Developer Tools (3)

**Idea 6: TokenLedger 🛠️ DEV TOOL**
As AI features scale, token spend silently balloons and nobody can say which feature is burning the budget. TokenLedger attributes LLM cost per feature/endpoint across models and providers, sets budget alerts, and flags prompts that suddenly got expensive.

**Idea 7: AgentTrace 🛠️ DEV TOOL**
When an AI agent gives a wrong answer, developers are stuck guessing which tool call or context mutation broke the chain. AgentTrace is an observability layer that replays a conversation step-by-step, showing every tool call, context-window change, and the exact moment things went sideways.

**Idea 8: MigraSafe 🛠️ DEV TOOL**
A bad database migration drops a column or locks a table and takes production down. MigraSafe runs in CI and reviews every migration before deploy, flagging destructive changes, long-running locks, and missing rollback plans with a plain-English risk report.

**Idea 9: ThockLog 📱 MOBILE APP**
Mechanical keyboard enthusiasts obsess over switch feel and "thock" sound but still track builds in spreadsheets and forum posts. **Idea 10: PaintWIP 📱 MOBILE APP**
Every tabletop wargamer has a "pile of shame" — unpainted minis stacked in boxes. PaintWIP tracks your backlog, logs paint recipes and color mixes per model, snaps WIP progress photos, and gamifies shrinking the pile with streaks and badges. Underserved hobby with a passionate community, near-zero competition, and a built-in meme ("pile of shame") that practically markets itself.
