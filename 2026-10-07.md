## General Ideas (5)

**Idea 1: BagClaim**
Airlines drag their feet on lost and delayed baggage compensation even though passengers are legally owed money (Montreal Convention / EU 261). BagClaim tracks your bag's last known location, auto-generates the correct claim letter with the right legal references, and escalates when the airline stalls. It takes a cut only when you get paid.

**Idea 2: TollTrack**
Transponder toll accounts quietly leak money through double-charges, misread plates, and unused accounts you forgot to close. TollTrack links all your toll/transponder accounts (E-ZPass, FasTrak, etc.), flags suspicious or duplicate charges, and files disputes with one tap. Aimed at commuters and rental-car users who get surprise toll bills weeks later.

**Idea 3: BillSwitch**
Most people pay more for electricity, gas, and internet than they need to, but comparing and switching providers is a maze of fine print. BillSwitch reads your current bill, finds cheaper plans in deregulated markets (and equivalent tariffs in the EU/UK), and handles the switch paperwork. It re-checks every renewal cycle so you never lapse back into an expensive default tariff.

**Idea 4: LunchAccount**
Parents have no visibility into what their kid is actually buying at the school cafeteria or when the meal account is about to go negative. LunchAccount syncs school meal-account balances, sends low-balance alerts, and shows a daily summary of purchases so parents can spot junk-food binges or a child skipping lunch. Handles multiple kids across multiple schools in one view.

**Idea 5: MedBillAudit**
An estimated 80% of hospital bills contain errors — duplicate charges, upcoding, or services never rendered. MedBillAudit ingests itemized bills and insurance EOBs, flags likely errors and overcharges, and generates a professional appeal letter tailored to your insurer and state. Flat fee per bill, no commission, because it's your money being clawed back.

## Developer Tools (3)

**Idea 6: BisectBot 🛠️ DEV TOOL**
When a test starts failing, devs waste hours manually binary-searching git history to find the culprit commit. BisectBot hooks into your CI, takes a failing test plus the last-known-good commit, and runs an automated git bisect across your build to pinpoint the exact commit and file that introduced the regression. Returns a blame-ready report with the diff and suggested fix context.

**Idea 7: PRBrief 🛠️ DEV TOOL**
Writing good PR descriptions, reviewer summaries, and changelog entries is manual, inconsistent, and frequently skipped. PRBrief reads the diff, infers intent from commit messages and code changes, and auto-generates a structured PR title, description, testing notes, and a changelog-ready one-liner. It also drafts the "what changed" comment for release notes so maintainers never hand-write them again.

**Idea 8: ComponentRadar 🛠️ DEV TOOL**
Large frontend codebases accumulate near-duplicate React components and inconsistent design tokens that nobody has time to catalog. ComponentRadar scans the codebase, clusters visually and structurally similar components, and flags duplicates, unused variants, and token/style drift against your design system. It gives teams a cleanup backlog with estimated risk per merge, so refactors are data-driven instead of vibes.
