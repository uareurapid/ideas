## General Ideas (5)

**Idea 1: GarageWatch**
Everyone has driven away wondering "did I actually close the garage door?" GarageWatch uses a cheap door sensor to confirm open/closed, sends a phone alert if you leave it open, and lets a neighbor or family member remotely see and close it. Turns a daily low-grade anxiety into one glance at your phone.

**Idea 2: MeterMinder**
People rack up parking tickets not from defiance but from losing track of the meter — and nobody can tell you what parking actually costs them each month. MeterMinder counts down your meter, nudges you to refeed it before it expires, and logs all your parking spend into a simple monthly total. A boring, universal pain solved before the ticket lands.

**Idea 3: CpapCare**
Millions of CPAP users quietly neglect cleaning, filter swaps, and mask replacement, then wonder why therapy stops working. CpapCare schedules cleaning and part-replacement reminders per machine, tracks compliance so you can see your own improvement, and flags when insurance allows a fresh mask. The maintenance nobody tells you about, for a condition nobody talks about.

**Idea 4: PuffPal**
Asthma and COPD patients constantly lose track of inhaler puffs remaining, expiry dates, and refill timing — and often discover they're empty mid-attack. PuffPal logs every dose, counts down remaining puffs per canister, and alerts you (and a caregiver) before you run dry or a script lapses. A safety net that lives in the puffer's pocket.

**Idea 5: SprinklerSmart**
Homeowners overwater lawns and gardens because sprinklers run on a dumb fixed schedule regardless of rain or season. SprinklerSmart reads local weather and evapotranspiration to tell you exactly when to run, for how long, and when to skip — cutting water bills and rescuing overwatered plants. Smart enough to be useful, simple enough to replace the dial timer.

## Developer Tools (3)

**Idea 6: SDKSync 🛠️ DEV TOOL**
Teams hand-write API clients and docs that drift from the spec within a week, then support tickets pour in from confused integrators. SDKSync generates typed SDKs (TypeScript, Python, Go) and reference docs straight from your OpenAPI spec, then watches for contract changes and opens a ready-to-merge PR to re-sync. Your SDK stops being the forgotten stepchild of the API.

**Idea 7: TestGap 🛠️ DEV TOOL**
A PR can pass CI with zero tests touching the changed lines, and the regression only shows up in production. TestGap shows exactly which changed lines lack coverage, ranks them by risk, and suggests the minimal tests to write before merge. It makes "did you test this?" a one-glance answer instead of a review argument.

**Idea 8: QuotaWatch 🛠️ DEV TOOL**
Indie apps quietly die when a third-party API (OpenAI, Stripe, SendGrid) hits a rate limit or quota mid-request, and teams only find out from angry users. QuotaWatch tracks your consumption and remaining headroom across every provider, alerts you before you hit limits, and logs the failures so you can renegotiate or cache before it bites. The quota dashboard every API-dependent product needs and nobody builds.
