## General Ideas (5)

**Idea 1: ParentPitstop**
Parents out with a baby never know if the café, mall, or museum has a clean changing table or a private nursing room until they're already there and desperate. ParentPitstop is a crowdsourced map and review app of nursing rooms and changing stations — verified, rated by other parents, and filterable by "has a chair," "actually clean," and "door locks."

**Idea 2: RentCredit**
On-time rent payments do nothing for your credit score while a missed one can wreck it. RentCredit reports your verified monthly rent payments to the major credit bureaus, so responsible renters build credit just by paying the rent they already pay — a simple, underserved gap between landlords, tenants, and lenders.

**Idea 3: InvoiceNudge**
Freelancers lose weeks of income silently chasing overdue invoices because they hate sending awkward reminders. InvoiceNudge sends polite, escalating payment reminders automatically, tracks when clients open them, and drops in ready-to-send small-claims and late-fee templates when friendly nudges stop working.

**Idea 4: FosterLoop**
Animal rescues run on chaotic spreadsheets and group chats to coordinate foster homes, supplies, vet runs, and pickup days. FosterLoop is a lightweight coordination hub where rescues list animals needing fosters, volunteers claim slots, and everyone sees who's bringing food, who's driving to the vet, and who's covering the weekend.

**Idea 5: OutageGuard**
When the power goes out, nobody remembers how long the fridge food is safe or whether the generator's been serviced. OutageGuard sends outage and extreme-weather alerts, starts a food-safety countdown for your fridge and freezer, and keeps generator maintenance and storm-prep checklists so you're ready before the lights go out.

## Developer Tools (3)

**Idea 6: RoboReview 🛠️ DEV TOOL**
More code than ever is written by AI assistants, and teams are merging it without knowing if it's secure or even correct. RoboReview automatically audits AI-generated PRs — flagging hallucinated APIs, injection risks, and license issues — before they reach a human reviewer, so "Copilot wrote it" stops being a liability.

**Idea 7: CodeClock 🛠️ DEV TOOL**
Developers have no honest idea where their week went, and freelancers under-bill because they forget half their context-switching. CodeClock quietly reconstructs your time from git activity, calendar, and Slack into a weekly breakdown (coding vs. review vs. meetings vs. interruptions), giving accurate invoices and a clear picture of what's actually eating your days.

**Idea 8: DocPulse 🛠️ DEV TOOL**
Teams write documentation blind — they can't see which pages users actually read, where they get stuck, or which tutorials cause support tickets. DocPulse adds lightweight analytics to your docs to show read-through, drop-off points, and search misses, so you fix the docs that matter instead of guessing.

## Mobile App Ideas (2)

**Idea 9: RockHound 📱 MOBILE APP**
Amateur geologists and fossil hunters have no good place to identify, log, and brag about their finds — existing rock ID apps are generic, and there's no collection-centric community. RockHound combines AI-assisted rock and fossil identification with a personal collection log and a feed for sharing your weirdest specimen. It's low-competition because the audience is small but obsessed, and it's built to go viral with "is this a fossil or just a weird rock?" arguments and before/after cleaning reveals.

**Idea 10: SceneSteal 📱 MOBILE APP**
Improv troupes, theater kids, and drama teachers need fresh warm-up games and scene prompts, but every troupe recycles the same five games. SceneSteal is a pocket generator of absurd scene prompts, random character/relationship/object combos, and Whose-Line-style games. It's a tiny, underserved niche with almost no dedicated app, and the prompts are inherently shareable and funny — "a mime who can only confess their feelings through interpretive dance at a DMV" sells itself on TikTok.
