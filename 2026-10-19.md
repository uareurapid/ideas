## General Ideas (5)

**Idea 1: LiftAlert**
When the only elevator in an apartment building breaks, residents get no reliable information and managers lose the repair history. LiftAlert lets neighbors report an outage in one tap, shows repair status and ETA, and keeps a timestamped log that managers can use to hold service vendors accountable.

**Idea 2: BusPulse**
School bus apps are often outdated or district-only, leaving parents unsure whether the bus is late, cancelled, or already gone. BusPulse lets parents on the same route crowdsource live status and get a push alert before they leave the house, with a one-tap "bus came early" report.

**Idea 3: ManualMash**
Half the furniture and appliances sold secondhand come without a manual, and manufacturer sites are a maze of discontinued model numbers. ManualMash lets users scan a model plate, pull community-uploaded manuals and repair notes, and add the missing steps themselves.

**Idea 4: PriorAuthPal**
Patients rarely know where an insurance prior authorization stands, and missed deadlines delay care for weeks. PriorAuthPal tracks submissions, flags response deadlines, and generates appeal letters from a plain-language questionnaire, keeping patient and clinic on the same timeline.

**Idea 5: BoxCycle**
Moving boxes get bought, used once, and thrown out while someone two streets away is buying the same boxes. BoxCycle is a neighborhood box library with deposits, pickup scheduling, and condition ratings, so boxes circulate until they fall apart.

## Developer Tools (3)

**Idea 6: FlakeCourt 🛠️ DEV TOOL**
Flaky tests quietly destroy trust in CI, but most teams just retry and move on. FlakeCourt mines CI history to score each test's flake rate, auto-quarantines the worst offenders, assigns an owner, and only lets a test back into the main suite after N clean runs.

**Idea 7: MigrationGuard 🛠️ DEV TOOL**
Database migrations are the scariest part of most deploys: a missing index or a table lock can take production down. MigrationGuard runs in CI, flags locking or destructive operations, estimates downtime, and suggests a safe expand/contract rewrite with a rollback plan.

**Idea 8: CFPilot 🛠️ DEV TOOL**
Developers who want to speak at conferences lose track of dozens of CFPs, deadlines, and talk versions across tabs and inboxes. CFPilot keeps a deadline calendar, stores reusable abstracts and bios, tracks submission status, and nudges you when a conference you missed opens next year.

## Mobile App Ideas (2)

**Idea 9: PumpkinPi 📱 MOBILE APP**
Giant-vegetable growers obsess over circumference, estimated weight, and show dates, but they are stuck with spreadsheets and Facebook groups. PumpkinPi logs measurements against growth curves, predicts final weight, schedules feeding, and generates shareable "veg flex" cards for the club. Niche: competitive giant pumpkin and marrow growers; low competition because generic garden apps ignore weigh-off rules and growth formulas. It is funny and viral because leaderboards, dramatic time-lapses, and public "my pumpkin is bigger than yours" cards beg to be shared.

**Idea 10: GreyLegion 📱 MOBILE APP**
Miniature painters all have a pile of unpainted plastic — the "grey legion" — and no honest way to track it. GreyLegion logs models bought versus painted, stores paint recipes, runs monthly pledges, and shames you gently with a friend leaderboard. Niche: Warhammer and tabletop miniature painters; low competition because existing apps focus on color matching, not backlog accountability. The shame-score stats and before/after reveals are inherently shareable, which makes it funny and viral inside hobby communities.
