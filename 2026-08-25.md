## General Ideas (5)

**Idea 1: AlignTrack**
Invisible-aligner and braces wearers constantly forget to switch trays on schedule and can't prove wear time to their orthodontist. AlignTrack logs daily wear hours, sends tray-switch reminders, and builds a photo timeline so patients hit their milestones and skip surprise re-treatment.

**Idea 2: BatteryBuddy**
Every household has a junk drawer of batteries and no idea which devices take what, or which rechargeables are dead. BatteryBuddy inventories your batteries and devices, flags which gadgets need fresh cells, and reminds you to recharge or recycle before they leak and ruin a remote.

**Idea 3: RehabRep**
Physical therapy only works if patients actually do the exercises at home, but they forget reps, sets, and pain levels between appointments. RehabRep logs each exercise session with guided form videos and pain tracking, then shares a clean progress report with the physio so recovery stays on track.

**Idea 4: FilterLog**
No one remembers when they last changed the fridge water filter, HVAC filter, Brita, vacuum, or air purifier filter. FilterLog keeps a per-appliance filter calendar with model-specific replacement intervals and reminders, cutting wasted money on over-replacement and the grossness of under-replacement.

**Idea 5: SpecSpot**
Reading-glasses households scatter four pairs around the house and lose all of them daily. SpecSpot lets family members photo-log where each pair was last seen (with one-tap updates), plus tracks lens prescription renewal dates, so "where are my glasses?" stops being a daily scavenger hunt.

## Developer Tools (3)

**Idea 6: MigrationGuard 🛠️ DEV TOOL**
Dangerous database migrations — dropping columns, silent data loss, or locking a huge table — still ship to production and take the app down. MigrationGuard runs as a PR-time CI check that flags risky schema changes, estimates lock/rewrite impact, and suggests safe two-step migration patterns before merge.

**Idea 7: SecretRotator 🛠️ DEV TOOL**
Teams accumulate API keys and secrets that are years old, reused across repos, or already sitting in git history. SecretRotator scans the codebase and commit history for aged, duplicated, or leaked credentials, then walks the team through a guided rotation workflow per provider so breaches stop compounding.

**Idea 8: ReferralOS 🛠️ DEV TOOL**
Indie SaaS founders want word-of-mouth growth but building an invite-a-friend rewards system from scratch is a slog. ReferralOS drops in a customer-to-customer referral engine — unique invite links, reward tiers, and anti-abuse/dupe detection — so happy users recruit the next cohort without founders hand-rolling the plumbing.

## Mobile App Ideas (2)

**Idea 9: NapSpot 📱 MOBILE APP**
A crowd-sourced map of the best semi-public places to nap — quiet library corners, office wellness rooms, shaded park benches, airport nap pods. Niche: exhausted office workers, students, and shift parents with no good app for "where can I crash for 20 minutes near here." Low competition because nobody maps napping as a first-class need, and the reviews ("quiet, but a guy was snoring in stall 3") write themselves.

**Idea 10: VendFinder 📱 MOBILE APP**
Locate and review vending machines near you — what's actually stocked, whether it takes card or eats your money, and if the C4 row is reliably empty. Niche: office workers, students, and station commuters who want a specific snack or a working machine. Low competition (vending maps barely exist), and the machine-specific drama and "haunted machine that eats dollars" reviews make it inherently shareable.
