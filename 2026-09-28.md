## Developer Tools (3)

**Idea 6: CacheBust 🛠️ DEV TOOL**
After a deploy, stale CDN/browser caches serve old assets and devs burn hours chasing "works on my machine" bugs that are just cache. CacheBust fingerprints your build, detects which assets are stale across CDN and browser, and triggers selective purges or cache-busting URL bumps so the new version actually ships.

**Idea 7: CSPLab 🛠️ DEV TOOL**
Content-Security-Policy headers are powerful but painful to write, and one wrong directive silently breaks your app. CSPLab generates a CSP from your actual site traffic, runs a report-only mode to catch violations, and suggests the tightest policy that won't break scripts, styles, or third-party embeds.

**Idea 8: VulnWatch 🛠️ DEV TOOL**
Dependency vulnerability alerts exist, but nobody turns a CVE into an actual fixed, tested PR. VulnWatch watches your lockfile, flags CVEs and EOL packages, and auto-opens upgrade PRs with a diff summary and a risk score — so security debt gets closed instead of just notified.
