**Idea 1: TattooAftercare**

A mobile tracker with studio-specific care plans, day-by-day healing milestones, photo logging of the healing stages, and a "is this normal or infected?" checklist — with one-tap messaging back to their artist. Turns a nerve-wracking 3-week healing window into a guided, reassuring ritual.

Bridges the gap between medical jargon and real patient understanding.
