## General Ideas (5)

**Idea 1: BreakerBuddy**
Almost every home has an unlabeled breaker panel, and tracing circuits means flipping switches and shouting across the house. BreakerBuddy guides a two-person workflow — one flips breakers while the other taps which outlets and fixtures die — and builds a labeled panel map you can print and stick on the door. It turns a frustrating guessing game into a 20-minute one-time task.

**Idea 2: ConsignTrack**
People who sell clothes and furniture through consignment and thrift stores lose track of what they dropped off, where it is, and how much they're owed. ConsignTrack logs each drop-off with photos, store terms, and payout percentages, then flags items that have sat unsold or payments that never arrived. A small but real money-and-memory leak for the reselling niche.

**Idea 3: ApplianceDecode**
Washing machines, dryers, dishwashers, and ovens are covered in cryptic cycle icons ("sanitize" vs "heavy duty" vs a squiggle) that nobody understands. ApplianceDecode lets you point your camera at a control panel and get a plain-English explanation of each cycle and when to actually use it. It solves a universal everyday confusion with zero learning curve.

**Idea 4: StorageAudit**
Millions of people pay monthly for storage units full of items they can no longer name, bleeding money for years. StorageAudit builds a photo inventory of what's in each unit, reminds you to revisit or downsize, and shows your total lifetime spend to trigger the "sell it or dump it" decision. It attacks a quiet, persistent household cost almost nobody tracks.

**Idea 5: RepairOrReplace**
When an appliance breaks, homeowners have no data to decide whether a $200 repair beats a $900 replacement. RepairOrReplace pulls typical repair costs, part availability, and average remaining lifespan to score "fix vs replace" in seconds. It fills a genuine decision gap for a decision everyone makes eventually.

## Developer Tools (3)

**Idea 6: DesignTok 🛠️ DEV TOOL**
Design systems constantly drift because Figma tokens and codebase tokens diverge silently, breaking brand consistency across products. DesignTok watches both sources, flags every mismatch (colors, spacing, type), and generates a ready-to-merge PR that re-syncs them. It eliminates one of the most common, least-talked-about sources of UI debt.

**Idea 7: SunsetKit 🛠️ DEV TOOL**
Deprecating an API or feature without breaking paying users is one of the scariest jobs in shipping. SunsetKit schedules the deprecation, announces it to affected users, tracks who's still on old versions, and auto-generates migration guides until the cutoff date. It turns "rip the band-aid off" into a calm, measurable process.

**Idea 8: ComplyKit 🛠️ DEV TOOL**
Enterprise customers increasingly demand SOC 2 before signing, but solo founders and small teams can't afford compliance consultants. ComplyKit automates evidence collection from AWS, GitHub, and Slack, generates policies, and walks you through the audit checklist. It unlocks enterprise deals for indie SaaS that would otherwise be priced out of the sale.

## Mobile App Ideas (2)

**Idea 9: Griswold 📱 MOBILE APP**
Every December, neighborhoods compete over the most over-the-top holiday light display — and everyone has an opinion. Griswold is a map-based app for photographing, rating, and sharing the gaudiest local light shows (and the saddest single-strand attempts). It's a seasonal, hyper-niche audience with almost no competition, and the mock-award angle gives it real shareable, viral potential each holiday season.

**Idea 10: SlapSwap 📱 MOBILE APP**
Sticker collectors — skate, graffiti, street-art, and "slap" culture — have no dedicated home to catalog, trade, and show off their hoards. SlapSwap is a visual collection tracker with a trading feed where users photograph stickers and offer swaps. It serves a small but obsessive subculture that's completely underserved by generic apps, and the colorful, oddly-specific collections are built for viral "look at this" posts.
