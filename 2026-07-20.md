## General Ideas (5)

**Idea 1: ReturnTracker**
Online returns are a mess — people forget what they sent back, lose tracking numbers, and miss refunds. ReturnTracker consolidates all your returns across every retailer, tracks refund status in real-time, and alerts you before return windows close. Simple peace of mind for the $800B+ return economy.

**Idea 2: BidMed**
Non-emergency medical procedures are a pricing black box — patients never know what they'll pay until the bill arrives. BidMed lets providers bid on your procedure (MRI, colonoscopy, dental work), giving transparent upfront pricing. Like Zocdoc meets reverse auction for healthcare.

**Idea 3: AlterMap**
Everyone owns clothes that need alterations, but finding a reliable tailor is shockingly hard — Google Maps reviews don't tell you who's good at taking in a suit vs hemming jeans. AlterMap is a dedicated directory of local tailors with reviews organized by alteration type, before/after photos, and price estimates.

**Idea 4: PolicyLens**
Insurance policies are dense legalese nobody reads until it's too late. PolicyLens uses AI to scan your policy documents and explain what's actually covered in plain language — before you need it. Upload any policy (home, auto, health, travel) and get a one-page "what you're actually covered for" summary.

**Idea 5: NeighborChef**
The cottage food economy is booming but there's no good platform for it. NeighborChef lets home cooks sell meals to neighbors — post tonight's menu, take orders from your block, and earn money doing what you'd cook anyway. Hyperlocal food marketplace for regular people, not restaurants.

## Developer Tools (3)

**Idea 6: SponsorMe 🛠️ DEV TOOL**
Open source maintainers struggle to monetize — writing sponsorship pitches and tracking down corporate sponsors is a skill most devs don't have. SponsorMe generates professional sponsorship proposals from your GitHub metrics (stars, downloads, dependent repos), suggests sponsorship tiers with benefits, and identifies companies already using your project who should be sponsoring you.

**Idea 7: CodeWhisper 🛠️ DEV TOOL**
PR descriptions lie — "just a small refactor" often touching 47 files. CodeWhisper compares your PR description against the actual code diff and flags discrepancies: undocumented breaking changes, missing migration steps, untested edge cases mentioned in comments but not covered. Catches the "I swear I tested this" moments before they reach production.

**Idea 8: DevRelMetrics 🛠️ DEV TOOL**
Developer relations teams fly blind — docs views live in one dashboard, GitHub stars in another, Discord activity somewhere else. DevRelMetrics unifies all DevRel KPIs into a single dashboard: API usage growth, community health scores, forum response times, docs engagement, and conversion from community member to paying customer. For teams that have DevRel but zero analytics.

## Mobile App Ideas (2)

**Idea 9: TextRegret 📱 MOBILE APP**
We've all sent texts we immediately regretted — angry rants, drunk confessions, 2AM "you up?" messages. TextRegret holds your drafted messages in a time-lock for a configurable cooldown period (15 min to 24 hours) before they actually send. Think of it as a breathalyzer lock, but for your thumbs. Niche: emotional regulation meets technology. Low competition — there are habit blockers and focus apps, nothing specifically for preventing impulsive digital communication. Highly shareable concept with massive meme potential.

**Idea 10: SnackRank 📱 MOBILE APP**

Japanese KitKat flavors, obscure regional potato chips, that weird dried squid from the truck stop in Nevada — they're all here. Niche: snack enthusiasts, road trip communities, and "weird food" content creators. Zero dedicated apps in this space, yet snack collecting/trading communities are surprisingly large on Reddit and TikTok. Built for virality through sheer weirdness — imagine the "gas station snack tier list" content this generates.
