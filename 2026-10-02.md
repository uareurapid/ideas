## General Ideas (5)

**Idea 1: WinterFuel**
Millions of rural homeowners heat with propane or heating oil and only realize they're low when the tank hits empty mid-blizzard. A small sensor + auto-refill service that watches your tank level, predicts burn rate from weather, and schedules a delivery before you ever run out. Solves a genuinely scary, recurring winter problem nobody's productized well.

**Idea 2: HearClear**
Hearing aids are confusing for seniors and stressful for their adult children — batteries die, Bluetooth pairing breaks, and nobody knows how to troubleshoot. An app that logs each device, reminds wearers when batteries are low, and lets a family member remotely see status and walk them through fixes. Aging population, real daily pain, almost no consumer-friendly competition.

**Idea 3: WellSafe**
The ~40 million Americans on private wells have to remember periodic water testing and septic pumping, but the schedule is invisible and easy to forget. A simple tracker that stores your last test/pump date, nudges you when the next one is due, and flags local contamination advisories. Deeply underserved rural niche with real health stakes.

**Idea 4: SpeechBuddy**
Speech therapy is expensive and progress stalls between weekly sessions because home practice is boring and untracked. A gamified at-home practice app for kids (and stroke survivors) that turns articulation drills into short games and shares progress with the therapist. Bridges a real gap between clinic and home.

**Idea 5: BallotPrep**
Local elections are confusing — most voters know the president but nothing about the judge, school board, or ballot measure they're voting on. A nonpartisan tool that summarizes every local candidate and ballot measure in plain language, based on official filings, so people can vote informed in 10 minutes. Timely, genuinely useful, and no honest competition at the hyper-local level.

## Developer Tools (3)

**Idea 6: DriftGuard 🛠️ DEV TOOL**
Infrastructure-as-code drift is a classic footgun: someone hand-edits the AWS/GCP console and now your Terraform state is a lie. DriftGuard continuously diffs your live cloud resources against your IaC config, alerts on out-of-band changes, and generates the exact revert or import command. Turns "works on my console" into a visible, fixable problem.

**Idea 7: RestoreCheck 🛠️ DEV TOOL**
Everyone has backups; almost nobody has tested them. RestoreCheck schedules automated "restore drills" that spin up your database/volume backup in a sandbox, verify integrity, and report a pass/fail before you actually need it. A CI-style safety net for the moment a real disaster hits, not the moment you discover the backup was corrupt.

**Idea 8: ChannelFit 🛠️ DEV TOOL**
Founders waste months guessing between SEO, Reddit, TikTok, cold email, and partnerships. ChannelFit scores your product and target audience against each acquisition channel and returns a ranked plan with a starter playbook. A "which channel should I even try" decision tool, distinct from the dozens of single-channel execution tools.

## Mobile App Ideas (2)

**Idea 9: SeaGlassLog 📱 MOBILE APP**
Beachcombers, sea glass hunters, and shell collectors are a passionate but completely underserved niche. The app logs each find with GPS, photos, rarity ratings, and tide/wind conditions so users learn which beaches and weather produce the best haul — and share trophy finds that make it inherently viral. Low competition (almost no dedicated beachcombing apps) and a built-in "look what I found" social loop.

**Idea 10: BuzzClock 📱 MOBILE APP**
Tracks your caffeine intake and shows your real-time caffeine level on a half-life curve — so you can see that your 4pm latte is still 25% active at midnight and forecast when you'll actually be able to sleep. Funny, genuinely useful for anyone who's ever lain awake cursing a coffee, and the "here's when you crash" chart is made to be screenshotted. Low competition in a space full of generic sleep trackers.
