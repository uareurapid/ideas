**Idea 6: PromptRevert 🛠️ DEV TOOL**
AI features break when you tweak prompts, and git diffs on large prompt templates are barely human-readable. PromptRevert versions your prompts with semantic diffing (showing what changed in behavior/output, not just text), lets you A/B test prompt variations against real production inputs side-by-side, and instantly roll back to any version when the new one starts hallucinating — like feature flags, but for your AI logic layer.

**Idea 7: DependencyPulse 🛠️ DEV TOOL**
Critical open-source dependencies go unmaintained, get hijacked in supply-chain attacks, or accumulate security vulnerabilities — and most teams find out from a Twitter thread three days late. DependencyPulse continuously monitors your package.json / requirements.txt / Cargo.toml and alerts you the moment a dependency is abandoned (no commits in 6 months), flagged as compromised, or hits a critical CVE — with suggested migration paths to actively maintained forks or alternatives.

**Idea 8: CommitContext 🛠️ DEV TOOL**
AI-generated commit messages are flooding repositories, but "fix bug" or "update code" tells reviewers nothing. CommitContext enriches sparse or AI-authored commits by scanning the actual diff, related PR discussion, and linked issues to auto-generate a structured commit narrative: what actually changed, why, which edge cases are affected, and what reviewers should focus on — turning cryptic commits into self-documenting history.
