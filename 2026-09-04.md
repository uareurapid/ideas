## General Ideas (5)

**Idea 1: RxRelay**
Keeping track of prescriptions across multiple pharmacies is a mess — people run out mid-refill, can't remember which script lives where, and get blindsided by non-transferable medications. RxRelay logs every prescription, warns you days before you'll run dry, and handles pharmacy-to-pharmacy transfer requests (flagging the ones that legally can't move).

**Idea 2: SharedFence**
Neighbors fight for months over who owns the fence, the overhanging tree, or the shared drain because there's no neutral record. SharedFence pulls local easement and property-line rules, generates polite-but-firm letter templates, and keeps a timestamped log of every exchange so the dispute never becomes "he said, she said."

**Idea 3: RenoLog**
Homeowners renovating have no single place to track quotes, permit deadlines, change orders, and contractor payments, so they get fleeced on scope creep. RenoLog is a plain-English renovation tracker that timelines every quote, payment, and inspection, and flags when a change order raises the total beyond your original budget.

**Idea 4: CellarLog**
Wine collectors track bottles in spreadsheets and forget which ones have hit their drinking window. CellarLog inventories your cellar with a photo, provenance, and value, then alerts you when a bottle is at peak — and reminds you what you actually paid before you open the "cheap" one.

**Idea 5: BoatLog**
Boat and RV owners forget the seasonal maintenance that costs thousands (winterizing, anodes, trailer bearings). BoatLog keeps a per-vessel service timeline, seasonalization checklists, and part/purchase history so spring launch never turns into a surprise four-figure repair.

**Idea 6: CLIPulse 🛠️ DEV TOOL**
Developers who ship CLI tools and SDKs have zero visibility into whether people actually install them, which commands get used, or where users silently drop off. CLIPulse adds lightweight analytics to your CLI, showing an install-to-daily-use funnel, command frequency, and error clustering so you can see what sticks and what confuses people.

**Idea 7: GuestPostFinder 🛠️ DEV TOOL**
Indie founders waste hours hunting for blogs, newsletters, and podcasts that accept guest posts or collabs — then more hours finding the right editorial contact. GuestPostFinder scans relevant sites by niche, surfaces ones that accept contributions, and hands you the editor's email plus their topic guidelines in one list.

**Idea 8: TriagePilot 🛠️ DEV TOOL**
Open-source maintainers drown in GitHub issues and can't tell the real bugs from the drive-by questions. TriagePilot auto-labels incoming issues, drafts a contextual first response, and routes each one to the contributor or maintainer most likely to handle it — turning a full-time triage job into a 10-minute review.
