## General Ideas (5)

**Idea 1: RetainerReflex**
Orthodontic patients — especially teens — constantly lose, forget, or skip their retainers and aligner trays, undoing thousands of dollars of treatment. RetainerReflex sends wear-streak reminders, tracks tray-change days, and gamifies "did you put it back in" check-ins, with a parent/orthodontist share view.

**Idea 2: LensCycle**
Contact lens wearers lose track of when to replace lenses, when to reorder, and when their prescription or eye exam expires. LensCycle tracks each lens pair's lifespan, nudges reorders before you run out, and flags expiring prescriptions and exam dates.

**Idea 3: InspectionMate**
Millions of drivers get ticketed or fail renewals because they lose track of vehicle registration, safety inspection, and emissions deadlines — a different beast from insurance, which people do track. InspectionMate keeps a countdown for every car, links renewal forms and test locations, and sends "don't get fined" reminders.

**Idea 4: SmokeCheck**
Smoke and CO detector batteries die quietly, and almost nobody remembers the 10-year detector expiry or monthly test schedule. SmokeCheck logs each detector's install date, sends battery/test reminders, and flags which units are past their replacement date.

**Idea 5: MedTakeback**
People hoard expired and unused medications but don't know how to safely dispose of them — flushing pollutes water, and take-back events are easy to miss. MedTakeback locates nearby DEA drug take-back locations and events, tells you what's safe to flush versus drop off, and reminds you before meds expire.

**Idea 6: SigPilot 🛠️ DEV TOOL**
Email signatures are a marketing surface almost every founder leaves static and wasted. SigPilot turns your signature into a live, auto-updating banner for your latest launch, changelog, or CTA across Gmail/Outlook, and A/B tests CTAs while measuring click-through from signatures.

**Idea 7: AdTrail 🛠️ DEV TOOL**
Indie SaaS builders can see competitors' launches and pricing changes, but not their ad creatives and copy. AdTrail monitors competitor Meta/TikTok/Google ad creatives, alerts you when copy or visuals change, and shows which angles are being scaled — a lightweight ad-spy tool for indie founders.

**Idea 8: DataBranch 🛠️ DEV TOOL**
Feature branches break against a shared database: migrations collide, test data leaks across branches, and resetting state wastes hours. DataBranch lets you branch your database alongside git branches — each feature branch gets an isolated, seeded DB, and schema changes merge like code — so shipping stays fast and safe.

Tiny audience, essentially zero dedicated apps, and genuinely shareable humor.
