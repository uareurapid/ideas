## General Ideas (5)

**Idea 1: FoundLink**
Lost wallets, keys, bikes, and pets get posted across scattered Facebook groups, lobby noticeboards, and building chats with no way to connect the dots. FoundLink is a lightweight neighborhood lost-and-found that matches "lost" posts against "found" posts by category, location, and photo, then alerts both parties the moment there's a hit.

**Idea 2: SchoolRunShare**
Coordinating the school run across working parents lives in chaotic WhatsApp threads with no-shows and last-minute swaps. SchoolRunShare turns it into a shared rotating roster with verified parents, automatic swap requests, and reminders so everyone knows exactly who is driving which kid today.

**Idea 3: BorrowLedger**
People lend tools, books, and money to friends, then forget about it or feel awkward chasing it back. BorrowLedger logs what went out, to whom, and when, then sends the lender a draft "just checking in" nudge at a chosen interval so nothing quietly disappears.

**Idea 4: TicketFight**
Contesting a parking or minor traffic ticket means deciphering jurisdiction-specific rules most people never bother with. TicketFight walks you through the valid grounds for appeal, pulls the right local form/template, and generates a submission-ready dispute letter in minutes.

**Idea 5: PawSplit**
After a breakup, shared pets get caught in the same messy custody fights as kids, but no tool exists for it. PawSplit gives exes a neutral shared calendar for pet handoffs, vet-visit splitting, and expense tracking, plus a message log so arrangements stay calm and documented.

## Developer Tools (3)

**Idea 6: MCPScan** 🛠️ DEV TOOL
Teams are wiring up MCP servers and agent tools faster than anyone can vet them, and tool descriptions are a new injection surface. MCPScan audits an MCP server's tool schemas and descriptions for prompt-injection and tool-poisoning patterns, flagging risky capabilities before it gets attached to a production agent.

**Idea 7: EnvDrift** 🛠️ DEV TOOL
Deploys break because an env var exists in code, is missing from `.env.example`, differs in staging, and was never set in prod. EnvDrift diffs variables referenced in code against every environment and config source, then blocks the deploy (or warns in CI) with the exact missing or renamed keys.

**Idea 8: LaunchCoupon** 🛠️ DEV TOOL
Indie founders fire off launch discounts across Product Hunt, newsletters, Reddit, and X but have no idea which channel actually converted paying users. LaunchCoupon generates unique per-channel promo codes and attributes redemptions (and revenue) back to the exact source, so the next launch budget goes where it works.
