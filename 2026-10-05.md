## General Ideas (5)

**Idea 1: DiveBuddy**
Scuba divers still juggle paper logbooks and struggle to find a trustworthy buddy when traveling. An app that logs dives, tracks certifications, and matches divers with local buddies by site, depth, and experience level. Underserved because most dive apps are clunky pro tools, not social.

**Idea 2: FiringBook**
Potters lose track of kiln firing schedules and glaze recipes across hundreds of pieces. A log app for firing curves, glaze formulas, and "what went wrong with this batch" notes. A passionate niche — ceramics studios and home potters have no polished dedicated tool.

**Idea 3: PreserveCheck**
Home canners risk serious food poisoning when processing times don't match their altitude. An app that logs each batch, auto-adjusts processing times for altitude, and flags jars nearing or past their safe date. A safety-driven niche with a built-in reason to return.

**Idea 4: SayMyName**
In diverse teams and events, hard-to-pronounce names get butchered constantly. A lightweight tool where anyone records their name's correct pronunciation plus a phonetic spelling, shareable as a link before meetings. Solves a real, everyday awkwardness with almost no competition.

**Idea 5: ReptiLog**
Reptile keepers track temperature, humidity, feeding, and shedding by hand in notebooks. A care log with species-specific temp/humidity targets and shed-and-feed reminders. A passionate, growing hobby with essentially no dedicated app.

**Idea 6: WarmPath 🛠️ DEV TOOL**
Founders know a warm intro beats cold outreach, but can't see who in their network actually knows a prospect. A tool that maps your LinkedIn/Twitter/email graph to surface the shortest warm path to any target. Turns "who do I know who knows them?" into a one-click answer.

**Idea 7: IaCDrift 🛠️ DEV TOOL**
Solo devs and small teams push Terraform or CloudFormation, then discover prod has drifted from the repo. A drift detector that diffs live cloud resources against your IaC and opens a fix PR. Reliability for teams that "can't justify a platform engineer yet."

**Idea 8: RightSize 🛠️ DEV TOOL**
Startups on Kubernetes over-provision CPU and memory and silently waste thousands a month. A tool that compares real usage against requests/limits and suggests safe per-workload reductions. A fresh cost angle for the "cloud bill is too high but no one has time to tune it" crowd.
