## General Ideas (5)

**Idea 1: PlanScope**
Planning applications within 500m of your home are buried in council portals nobody checks. PlanScope watches your local authority's planning list and sends a plain-English alert ("two-storey extension at No. 14, comments close 12 Dec") with a two-click observation form. €5/month per address; both objectors and estate agents pay for it.

**Idea 2: NewPatient**
Finding a dentist or GP actually accepting new patients means ringing fourteen surgeries. A crowdsourced live-status map where patients tap "registered here" or "turned away today", with freshness decay so stale entries fade out. Practices pay for a verified "accepting patients" badge.

**Idea 3: RainCheck**
Swim, music and dance schools lose money and goodwill on missed lessons because make-up credits live in a notebook. RainCheck gives each student a credit balance, lets parents self-book into spare slots, and expires credits per the school's own policy. €29/month per lesson business.

**Idea 4: ColourCard**
Clients lose their hair colour formula the moment their stylist moves salons, and stylists inherit clients with no history. A private formula passport — developer, ratio, timing, photos, patch-test notes — owned by the client and shared only with their chosen stylist. Sticky because it belongs to the client, not the salon.

**Idea 5: CottageShare**
Co-owned holiday homes and boats run on a WhatsApp group and a spreadsheet, so someone always feels they pay more and stay less. Shared booking calendar, per-night cost allocation, turnover checklist, and a year-end fairness score. Aimed at the fast-growing co-ownership market that Pacaso-style platforms ignore at the small end.

## Developer Tools (3)

**Idea 6: FleetVersion 🛠️ DEV TOOL**
If you ship self-hosted or on-prem software you have no idea which customers are three versions behind until they file a bug. A tiny beacon SDK plus dashboard shows version distribution, flags installs missing security patches, and drafts per-customer upgrade emails. Sells itself to any B2B devtool with a Docker install.

**Idea 7: InboxProof 🛠️ DEV TOOL**
Transactional emails look fine in your browser and break in Outlook 2019, dark mode, and Gmail clipping. InboxProof renders every template across 20 real clients on each CI run and posts a visual diff to the PR. Catches the "receipt table collapses in Outlook" bug before a customer does.

**Idea 8: InstallDoctor 🛠️ DEV TOOL**
Self-hosted products lose trials in the first ten minutes of installation. InstallDoctor runs your documented install on clean Ubuntu/Debian VMs nightly, measures time-to-first-successful-request, and files a GitHub issue the moment a step breaks. It's CI for your README.

## Mobile App Ideas (2)

**Idea 9: NextRound 📱 MOBILE APP**
Nobody remembers whose round it is, and the person who does remember is always the one who's owed. Tap once per round, see a live leaderboard of who is owed a pint, and get a polite nudge when it's your turn. Niche: pub-going friend groups in Ireland/UK/Australia; low competition because payment apps solve splitting, not round etiquette; funny because it publicly names the freeloader.

**Idea 10: BridgeShame 📱 MOBILE APP**
Duplicate bridge clubs still score on paper or 1990s software, then argue about the hand afterwards. Phone scoring for a table or a whole club, plus a post-session replay of where it went wrong in plain English — and a running "partner blame index" for laughs. Niche: millions of club bridge players; low competition because Bridge Base Online serves online play, not the church-hall Tuesday night; the partner-blame scoreboard is the shareable hook.
