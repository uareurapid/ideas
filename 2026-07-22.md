## General Ideas (5)

**Idea 1: SeniorTechBuddy**
A marketplace connecting tech-savvy helpers (teens, students, freelancers) with seniors who need device setup, app tutorials, and troubleshooting. Background-checked, hourly rates, available in-person or remote. The 65+ demographic is the fastest-growing smartphone segment and massively underserved — most tech support assumes you already know the basics.

**Idea 2: SocialScrub**
An AI-powered audit of your entire social media history — flags posts you might regret leaving up: outdated hot takes, cringey old photos, angry rants from 2014, anything that could surface in a job interview or date Google. Think "spring cleaning for your digital past." People are increasingly paranoid about old posts resurfacing, yet nobody wants to manually scroll through 10 years of tweets.

**Idea 3: BuildAlert**
Aggregates city building permit data to alert you about upcoming construction projects near your home or commute. Know about the 6-month road-widening project or the new apartment complex before the jackhammers start — not after. City permit data is public but scattered across municipal websites nobody checks. Proactive, not reactive.

**Idea 4: FoodSplit**
Group food delivery bills are always a mess with split items, tax, delivery fees, and tip. Snap a photo of the DoorDash/UberEats receipt, tag each item to a person, and it calculates exactly what everyone owes — including proportional tip and delivery. Solves the "wait, who got the extra guac?" argument forever.

**Idea 5: DrivewayPark**
Homeowners near stadiums, concert venues, and event spaces rent their driveways for event parking. Pre-book spots, set your own rates, list walking distance to the venue. Airbnb for event parking. Venue parking is $40+ and always full — neighbors with empty driveways are sitting on cash.

## Developer Tools (3)

**Idea 6: BadgeStack 🛠️ DEV TOOL**
Auto-scans your repo to detect your actual tech stack (from package.json, Cargo.toml, go.mod, Dockerfile, etc.) and generates beautiful, coordinated README badges for everything you're actually using. Not another manual badge designer — it discovers your stack automatically, updates when dependencies change, and generates a consistent visual style. Developers waste 20 minutes making badges look nice; this takes 2 seconds.

**Idea 7: LocalizeLint 🛠️ DEV TOOL**
A CI-ready CLI that scans your codebase for hardcoded strings, missing i18n keys, inconsistent interpolation patterns, and untranslated locales. Flags issues before they hit production. Most teams don't think about localization until launch week — this makes it a continuous hygiene check, like ESLint but for i18n. Plugs into GitHub Actions, catches the "oops we shipped English-only to Japan" moment before it happens.

**Idea 8: ColdEmailLab 🛠️ DEV TOOL**
An A/B testing platform specifically for developer-founders doing cold outreach. Write variant email templates, send small batches, track open and reply rates, and iterate. Shows you which subject line, tone, and CTA actually gets responses from prospects. Most devs send 50 cold emails, get 0 replies, and give up — this helps them find the version that works instead of guessing.
