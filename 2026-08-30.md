## General Ideas (5)

**Idea 1: PoolCare**
Pool owners juggle chlorine, pH, and shock schedules with no simple tracker, which leads to green water and expensive chemical guessing. A simple app that reads test-strip results, tells you exactly what to add, and reminds you weekly based on weather and usage.

**Idea 2: BarnBook**
Horse owners and small barns still run on paper feed charts and whiteboards, and things get missed whenever staff changes. A lightweight stable app that tracks feeding, turnout, farrier/vet visits, and medication for each horse, shared live with barn staff.

**Idea 3: PrintFleet**
3D-printing hobbyists and small print farms constantly lose track of filament spools, failed prints, and machine maintenance. An app that logs filament inventory, tracks print jobs, and flags which printer is about to jam or run out of material.

**Idea 4: KeyRing**
Nobody remembers who has a copy of their house keys — spares quietly circulate among friends, cleaners, and ex-roommates. A simple ledger of every key you've handed out, with reminders to collect keys when a lease or relationship ends, plus a "who to call" locksmith log.

**Idea 5: SoleMinder**
Quality shoes, boots, and sneakers need resoling, conditioning, and rotation, but people just wear them until they fall apart. A care-and-repair tracker that logs wears, reminds you when to condition leather or resole, and points you to a local cobbler before a beloved pair is ruined.
