## General Ideas (5)

**Idea 1: VetClaim**
Veterans lose disability benefits every year because they miss filing deadlines, misfile evidence, or give up on appeals. VetClaim is a plain-language navigator that tracks every VA claim, flags upcoming deadlines, organizes medical evidence, and shows exactly what to submit at each stage of an appeal. Think "TurboTax for VA disability," built for people who'd rather not deal with a 100-page form.

**Idea 2: SolarScore**
Homeowners can't tell if their solar panels are underperforming, and they leave SREC (solar renewable energy credit) money on the table. SolarScore compares your panels' actual production to what your system should produce given your roof, weather, and equipment, then flags dirty panels, inverter failures, and unclaimed credits. It turns "is my solar working?" from a gut feeling into a number.

**Idea 3: SepticSmart**
Rural and suburban homeowners forget to pump their septic tanks until there's a $5,000 backup. SepticSmart sends pumping and inspection reminders, logs service history, and teaches new homeowners what actually can't be flushed. Simple insurance against the most expensive plumbing mistake nobody thinks about.

**Idea 4: LittleFreeMap**
Little Free Libraries and community pantries are everywhere, but there's no map showing what's stocked or what's needed. LittleFreeMap lets stewards log inventory and lets neighbors find nearby boxes, see "this pantry needs canned beans," and drop off exactly what's requested. Turns scattered goodwill into a functioning neighborhood network.

**Idea 5: TurnoverCheck**
Airbnb and short-term-rental hosts juggle back-to-back guest turnovers and constantly catch the cleaner's mistakes after the guest has already complained. TurnoverCheck gives cleaners a photo-verified checklist (every room, every reset item) and sends the host a timestamped proof-of-done before check-in. It turns "did they actually restock the towels?" into a yes/no with receipts.

## Developer Tools (3)

**Idea 6: FirstIssue 🛠️ DEV TOOL**
Developers want to build a reputation through open-source contributions but can't find approachable issues, and maintainers can't recruit new contributors. FirstIssue matches a developer's languages and skill level to "good first issue" tickets across repos, then tracks a contribution streak they can put on their portfolio. A contributor marketplace for the OSS long tail.

**Idea 7: DesignDiff 🛠️ DEV TOOL**
The most common designer complaint is "the build doesn't match the mockup," and it's found too late. DesignDiff diffs the shipped UI against the Figma source and flags visual drift — wrong spacing, colors, missing states, misaligned text — in the PR, before it ships. A CI step that keeps engineering honest against design.

**Idea 8: CopyLint 🛠️ DEV TOOL**
Engineers ship products with inconsistent microcopy, missing error states, and jargon that confuses users. CopyLint scans your app's UI strings and flags tone drift, broken empty states, and unhelpful error messages, with suggested rewrites. It's ESLint, but for the words inside your product.

## Mobile App Ideas (2)

**Idea 9: MenaceMap 📱 MOBILE APP**
Runners, cyclists, and dog walkers get genuinely attacked by territorial geese, swans, and protective dogs, and nobody warns them. MenaceMap lets users drop pins for aggressive animals with a threat level and seasonality, then routes you around the danger. Niche (urban outdoors people), near-zero competition, and endlessly shareable — everyone has a goose story, and "avoid the murder-goose on the river path" writes its own virality.

**Idea 10: StrayCart 📱 MOBILE APP**
Abandoned shopping carts are a weird civic nuisance every city deals with, and nobody coordinates them. StrayCart lets you photograph and geo-tag a stranded cart, notifies the store or city, and awards "cart bounty" points for rescues with a local leaderboard. Absurd-but-real, zero competition, and the gamified "leave no cart behind" streak is exactly the kind of dumb-fun thing people post about.
