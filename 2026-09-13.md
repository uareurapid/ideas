## General Ideas (5)

**Idea 1: StableSync**
Horse owners and boarding barns juggle farrier visits, deworming schedules, feed changes, and blanket swaps across multiple animals and shared staff. A shared barn log with reminders and a care calendar keeps owners, grooms, and vets on the same page. Underserved niche with real, recurring pain.

**Idea 2: RefCoord**
Youth and adult rec leagues still schedule referees and officials through spreadsheets, text threads, and messy Venmo payouts. A tool that handles availability, game-day assignment, no-show tracking, and per-game payment. High recurring need, almost no dedicated software.

**Idea 3: GutSafe**
People on low-FODMAP, low-histamine, or other elimination diets can't tell if a restaurant dish or packaged food is safe without interrogating staff or decoding labels. A crowdsourced finder of "safe" menu items and grocery products filtered by specific trigger. Small but highly motivated, underserved community.

**Idea 4: PracticeLoop**
Music teachers assign practice but can't see what students actually did between lessons, and parents can't tell if "I practiced" is true. A shared practice log where teachers assign goals, students log minutes and reps, and parents get a lightweight view. Turns a vague weekly ritual into accountability.

**Idea 5: PawMemorial**
When a pet dies, owners face decisions (cremation, paw prints, ashes, keepsakes, at-home euthanasia) during peak grief while vendors upsell them. A calm, step-by-step guide and vendor checklist that removes the sales pressure. Quiet, real, and currently ignored niche.

## Developer Tools (3)

**Idea 6: PostmortemForge 🛠️ DEV TOOL**
Engineering teams dread writing incident postmortems, so the "why it happened" reflection gets skipped. A tool that ingests the incident timeline (deploys, alerts, chat, logs) and drafts a blameless postmortem with contributing factors and action items. Saves hours and makes retrospectives actually happen.

**Idea 7: ReviewLoad 🛠️ DEV TOOL**
PRs stall because reviewers are overloaded or the wrong people get pinged, creating review bottlenecks and single points of failure. A tool that maps code ownership and reviewer availability to auto-assign the right reviewer with capacity. Keeps small teams shipping without a review sheriff.

**Idea 8: DeviceBay 🛠️ DEV TOOL**
Indie mobile devs can't afford device farms, so they test on one phone and ship bugs on the rest. A peer-to-peer marketplace to rent idle test phones and tablets from other devs by the hour, with screenshots and logs baked in. Turns the "works on my device" problem into cheap, real-device coverage.
