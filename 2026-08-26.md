## General Ideas (5)

**Idea 1: RefReffy**
Youth sports referees juggle scattered assignments, cash or PayPal payments, and untracked mileage. RefReffy logs every game, tallies owed pay (and flags who still hasn't paid), and auto-computes tax-deductible mileage so officials stop losing money to bad bookkeeping.

**Idea 2: FlockLog**
Backyard chicken keepers have no simple way to track egg counts, flock health, or feed costs across seasons. FlockLog is a lightweight log where owners record eggs per hen, molts, and treatments, then spot production dips or illness early — a growing urban-homestead niche with almost no dedicated software.

**Idea 3: StoveLog**
Wood stove and fireplace owners forget sweep dates and can't tell if their firewood is dry enough to burn safely. StoveLog tracks creosote buildup, logs each sweep, and gives a quick wood-moisture check so users avoid chimney fires — an active r/woodstoving community with nothing purpose-built.

**Idea 4: SpotLift**
A stain sets in the seconds you waste Googling what to do. SpotLift is instant stain first-aid: snap a photo, identify the stain and fabric, and get the immediate do-this-now step before it becomes permanent — an everyday problem no one has made truly instant.

**Idea 5: AnglerLog**
Recreational anglers struggle to remember what worked where, and to stay compliant with changing size and season rules. AnglerLog logs catches with GPS, conditions, and lure, and overlays local regulations so a great spot doesn't turn into a fine.

## Developer Tools (3)

**Idea 6: BusFactor 🛠️ DEV TOOL**
When one engineer leaves, a whole subsystem often becomes unmaintainable. BusFactor maps git history into a "knowledge concentration" heatmap showing which files and features depend on a single person, so teams can spread knowledge before the bus hits.

**Idea 7: FeatureGrave 🛠️ DEV TOOL**
Every product is full of features nobody uses, but teams are scared to delete them. FeatureGrave correlates analytics events with code paths to surface abandoned features — zero usage in 90 days — with a kill checklist and rollback plan, so shipping less becomes safe.

**Idea 8: ReviewCycle 🛠️ DEV TOOL**
Slow code review quietly stalls every team, yet no one can point to the bottleneck. ReviewCycle tracks PR review latency, time-to-first-comment, and who's blocking, then sets SLA alerts and per-repo dashboards to unstick the merge queue.
