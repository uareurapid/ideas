## General Ideas (5)

**Idea 1: ShotLog**
Families juggle vaccination records for kids, adults, and travel across scattered paper cards, school forms, and clinic portals, then scramble every time a school or trip demands proof. ShotLog is a single household immunization passport — log every shot with date and lot number, get boosters and travel-vaccine reminders, and export a clean record to hand to any school, camp, or travel clinic. Turns "where did we put the yellow card?" into a one-tap share.

**Idea 2: CompostCue**
People start composting enthusiastically, then abandon it because they can't remember what's compostable, forget to turn the pile, and end up with a stinking, fruit-fly-infested bin. CompostCue is a home composting guide that identifies what goes in (photo or search), sets tumble/turn reminders per bin, and tracks temperature and moisture so the pile actually becomes usable soil instead of a science experiment gone wrong.

**Idea 3: PieceSwap**
Jigsaw puzzle enthusiasts finish a 2,000-piece puzzle and face an awkward question: buy another, or swap with someone? PieceSwap lets puzzlers log completed puzzles with photo and piece count, list them for local trades, and rate puzzle brands by quality — turning a closet full of "already done" puzzles into a rotating library within a passionate, fast-growing hobby community.

**Idea 4: ScopeLog**
Amateur astronomers spend half their observing night fighting clouds and light pollution because they can't easily see when a clear, dark window is coming or log what they saw. ScopeLog combines a dark-sky forecast with an observing journal — log targets, eyepieces, and seeing conditions, get alerts when your location hits clear skies, and build a personal record of every object you've bagged across the seasons.

**Idea 5: ScaleLog**
Reptile and terrarium keepers track feeding schedules, shed cycles, humidity, and temperature in messy notes, and a single missed feeding or humidity drop can sicken an animal. ScaleLog is a care tracker for snakes, geckos, and other exotics — log feedings and weight, track sheds and breeding, and get humidity/temperature alerts per enclosure, plus morph lineage records for breeders. A deeply underserved, passionate niche that currently lives in spreadsheets.

## Developer Tools (3)

**Idea 6: SecQ 🛠️ DEV TOOL**
Every B2B SaaS deal stalls on the same vendor security questionnaire — 200+ questions about SOC 2, HIPAA, encryption, and data retention that founders re-answer by hand for every prospect. SecQ ingests your existing security docs and policies, auto-fills any vendor questionnaire, and flags the gaps you'd fail on before the prospect does. Turns a week of compliance drudgery into a 10-minute export.

**Idea 7: ColdStart 🛠️ DEV TOOL**
Serverless functions and mobile apps both die on cold starts — Lambda init latency, slow app launch, bloated imports — and developers only discover it after users complain. ColdStart profiles cold-start time across your functions and app launch, pinpoints the exact culprit (heavy imports, eager initialization, oversized bundles), and suggests concrete fixes with before/after timing. The performance tool that targets the specific first-impression latency nobody benchmarks.

**Idea 8: ParityCheck 🛠️ DEV TOOL**
Teams ship features to web first, and mobile quietly falls behind until a customer files a ticket for a feature that "just doesn't exist on the app." ParityCheck scans your feature flags, release notes, and codebases across web, iOS, and Android, and produces a living feature-parity matrix that flags shipped-but-missing functionality and silently diverging behavior. Catches cross-platform drift before your customers do.
